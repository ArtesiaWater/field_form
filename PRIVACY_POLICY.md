# Privacy Policy for FieldForm

**Last Updated:** June 3, 2026

## Introduction

FieldForm is a mobile application that enables users to organize locations, perform measurements, and optionally share data with other users through user-configured FTP servers.

We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how FieldForm handles data.

## Information We Collect

FieldForm does **not collect, transmit, store, or process user-generated data on servers operated by the developer**.

Specifically, FieldForm does not collect:

* Location data created by users
* Measurement data entered or recorded by users
* Project data
* User accounts or profile information
* Contact information
* Data shared between users through FTP servers

All such data remains under the control of the user unless the user chooses to share it through their own FTP server.

## User Data Storage

Locations, measurements, and related project data are stored locally on the user's device unless the user chooses to export or share the data.

Users are responsible for managing, backing up, and protecting their own data.

## Data Sharing via FTP

FieldForm allows users to connect to and use FTP servers of their choosing to share locations and measurements with other users.

When using this feature:

* The FTP server is selected, configured, and controlled by the user.
* Data transferred through FTP servers is not routed through or stored on systems operated by the developer.
* The developer does not have access to files stored on FTP servers used by FieldForm users.
* The privacy and security of data stored on FTP servers are governed by the policies and practices of the FTP server operator.

Users should carefully evaluate the security and privacy practices of any FTP server they choose to use.

## Crash Reports and Diagnostics

FieldForm does not use its own analytics or crash-reporting services.

However, when FieldForm is distributed through application stores such as Google Play, the platform provider may collect technical diagnostic information, crash reports, and performance data and make such information available to the developer to help improve application stability and reliability.

This information is provided by the platform operator and is subject to the platform operator's privacy policies and terms. The developer does not use this information to identify individual users and does not combine it with user-generated location or measurement data.

## Permissions

FieldForm may request device permissions that are necessary for application functionality, including:

* Location access, if required for location-based measurements
* File access for importing, exporting, or managing data

These permissions are used solely to provide the requested functionality on the user's device.

## Third-Party Services

FieldForm does not use third-party analytics, advertising networks, or cloud services operated by the developer to collect user data.

Users may choose to connect FieldForm to third-party FTP services. Any interaction with such services is subject to the privacy policies and terms of those providers.

## Data Security

Because user-generated data is not collected or stored on servers operated by the developer, users retain primary responsibility for securing:

* Their devices
* Their backups
* Their FTP server credentials
* Any data they choose to share with others

Users are encouraged to use strong passwords and appropriate security measures for any FTP server used with FieldForm.

## Children's Privacy

FieldForm is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

Because FieldForm does not collect user-generated personal information, the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

## Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be submitted through the FieldForm GitHub repository.
